# Welcome to Zag Store

**A new chapter in iOS app discovery, signing, and sideloading.**

Zag Store is an independent, community-driven iOS project building upon the foundations of Feather, with a focus on a refined native experience, accessible app installation, and open-source collaboration.

Our ambition is to make alternative app distribution on iOS more intuitive, reliable, and accessible to everyone.

### Built on open-source foundations

Zag Store wouldn't exist without the work of the original Feather developers and the wider open-source community.

We recognise and appreciate the people whose contributions helped establish those foundations.

As Zag Store continues to evolve, we're committed to preserving that spirit of collaboration while developing new features, infrastructure, and experiences of our own.

### An invitation to the Feather community

To the developers and contributors who helped shape Feather: **you're warmly invited to be part of Zag Store's next chapter.**

Whether you'd like to contribute code, share ideas, report issues, review changes, or simply follow the project's development, you're welcome here.

Participation is entirely voluntary, and joining our organisation does not imply any obligation to contribute.
